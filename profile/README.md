# V0 Visualizer

### Your listening history has a story.

Discover the patterns. Revisit the favourites. Find your people.

---

V0 Visualizer is an independent music analytics and social platform built around Last.fm. We turn your listening history into a personal space for exploring your taste, rediscovering music, and connecting with other listeners.

From the track playing right now to the artists that have stayed with you for years, V0 brings your music into focus.

## Explore your music

| Experience | What you can do |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Listening insights** | Explore your top artists, albums and tracks, follow your listening habits, and see how your taste changes over time. |
| **V0Rooms** | Listen together in live rooms with synchronised playback, a shared DJ queue, reactions and chat. |
| **TasteMatch** | Compare music taste, discover shared favourites, and find the artists that connect you. |
| **Story Composer & artwork mosaics** | Turn your listening history into personalised visuals made for sharing. |
| **The Wall & friends** | Explore the community's current listening and keep up with the people you follow. |
| **Achievements** | Mark listening milestones, collect badges, and follow your progress. |

## Made for people who love listening

Music statistics can tell you what you played. We want to help you explore what those plays mean to you: the albums you return to, the way your taste evolves, and the music you share with others.

V0 Visualizer is free to use and independently built in Türkiye. Community feedback helps shape what comes next.
