V0 Visualizer turns your **Last.fm listening history** into a personal music space: rich insights, visual stories, and shared listening experiences. From the song playing right now to the albums you've loved for years, explore the music that makes your taste your own.

**Keep up with your people.** Follow friends and explore [The Wall](https://v0visualizer.com/the-wall) to see what the community is listening to.

**Celebrate the journey.** Collect achievements, unlock badges, and mark the listening milestones that make your archive unique.

## Independently built. Shaped by listeners.

V0 Visualizer is free to use and made in Türkiye. We build for people who love music, and community feedback helps decide what comes next.

Have an idea, found a bug, or just want to talk music? Our [Discord community](https://discord.gg/FpbNECYT5M) is the place for feedback, announcements and release conversations.
